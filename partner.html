Partner with Glo

Music with a mission: serving people in need and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Glo's mission is to partner with organizations that serve people in need, including homeless shelters, women's shelters and programs, rehabilitation centers, youth programs and youth choirs, programs for troubled youth, people facing medical challenges, and children without parents.

Together, we host events, raise funds, and perform. A portion of the proceeds raised goes to the partner organization, and through every partnership, Glo shares the message of Jesus Christ. Glo is working toward becoming a nonprofit organization to carry this mission further.

What a partnership can look like

  • Fundraising events
  • Performances at your events
  • Outreach and evangelism at your events
  • Other humanitarian efforts we can do together
How it works. Partnership events are free for qualifying organizations, and Glo reviews every request based on availability and the nature of the event. Details are agreed on with each organization before the event.
Contributions made on the Contribute page support Glo's ministry. A portion of the proceeds raised at partnered events goes to the partner organization.
Glofro LLC is not a registered nonprofit. Contributions are not tax-deductible.
