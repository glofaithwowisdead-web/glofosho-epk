Her story

A St. Louis native, choir director, and gospel artist putting Scripture to song for Dallas and beyond.

Gloretha Brown, known as Glo, is a St. Louis, Missouri native, gospel artist, and Youth Choir Director serving in Dallas, Texas. Her life and music are rooted in serving the true and living God, Jesus Christ, living according to His Word, and studying to show herself approved unto God.

Glo believes faith without works is dead, and that God-given talents should be used for His purpose. Through Truth Music, her mission is to put God's Word to music, allowing people to hear, learn, remember, and sing the Scriptures.

What is Truth Music? Truth Music is scripturally based music: songs built on the actual words of the Bible, verbatim, rather than freely written lyrics. Musically, Glo's sound is contemporary R&B and urban Christian music.

Her three Scripture-based singles, "Psalm 150 – Praise the Lord," "Teach Me" (Psalm 119:33-40), and "I'm a Winner" (Proverbs 31), are out now. Her EP, The Scriptures, follows late 2026.

This ministry isn't about recognition. It's about using what God has given her to plant His Word through music and lift up the name of Jesus Christ.
