Her story

A St. Louis native, choir director, and gospel artist putting Scripture to song for Dallas and beyond.

Gloretha Brown, affectionately known as Glo, is a St. Louis, Missouri native, gospel artist, and Youth Choir Director serving in Dallas, Texas. Her life and music are rooted in serving the true and living God, Jesus Christ, living according to His Word, and studying to show herself approved unto God.

Glo believes faith without works is dead, and that God-given talents should be used for His purpose. Through Truth Music, her mission is to put God's Word to music, allowing people to hear, learn, remember, and sing the Scriptures.

In September 2026, Glo released three Scripture-based singles: "Psalm 150 – Praise the Lord," "Teach Me," inspired by Psalm 119:33-40, and "I'm a Winner," inspired by Proverbs 31. Her EP follows on October 31, 2026.

For Glo, this ministry isn't about recognition — it's about using what God has given her to help plant His Word through music and lift up the name of Jesus Christ. Her prayer is that Truth Music finds an audience open to hearing, learning, and singing the Scriptures, giving God's Word another avenue to reach hearts.

Her prayer is to one day hear, "Well done, thou good and faithful servant." — Matthew 25:21
